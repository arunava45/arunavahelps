How I’d use this in a real role

In a live environment, this dashboard would be wired to scheduled exports or an API to help CS / Support / Partner Ops leadership:

  • Track escalation rate by month and detect problematic release windows.
  • Identify top recurring root-causes to prioritise SOPs, training, or product fixes.
  • Monitor resolution time and intervene when SLAs are at risk.
